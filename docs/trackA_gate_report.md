# Track A gate report: boundary-aware link prediction (H-A) — 2026-10-13

Protocol: docs/trackA_link_prediction.md. Every number below is generated by `experiments/trackA/report.py`
(`results/trackA/T_A.md`) from `results/trackA/runs.jsonl`. Runs with uncommitted code (`-dirty`) are excluded. All runs
are local (WSL Spark 3.5 / Delta 3.2 for Phases 0–2, one RTX A5000 for training). Test split, 3 seeds each.

## 1. Decision
**GO.** H-A says restoring boundary context closes ≥ 50% of the Stage 3a → full-graph gap on the union AUC, on Reddit
and ogbn-products, under both negative schemes, at ≤ 2× Stage 3a's bytes read. It holds at the pre-registered
100-epoch protocol:
- **M2 with the halo capped at R = 1 × members** (the halo vertices with the most edges into the unit) meets it on
  both datasets: Reddit 57% (global) and no regression on matched negatives at 1.61× bytes; products 104% / 225% at
  1.53× bytes. It also meets it on the third dataset, ogbn-arxiv, where the full halo is already within budget (M2 110% /
  109% at 1.92×).
- **M5, late fusion** (Stage 3a members only + one shared decoder on [h_local, h_macro, x]) meets it on all three
  datasets at about Stage 3a's I/O: Reddit 78% at 1.13× bytes, products 69% / 132% at 1.05×, arxiv 94% / 137% at 1.04×.
  On cross-unit links it is weaker than the halo, though (products 0.815 vs 0.955 for Phase 3).
- **LakeGRL's Phase 3 as built** (M1b, full 1-hop halo) meets it on arxiv and products but **not on Reddit**, where the
  halo reads 3.10× Stage 3a's bytes (5.3× its rows).

## 4. With tuned budgets (validation-chosen epochs; robustness, not the gate)
Budgets were chosen on the validation split (seed 0) per method from {100, 300, 1000} (M0) and {100, 300} (units), by
the cross-unit AUC with global negatives. So with tuned budgets the halo beats the full graph on Reddit and products,
and arxiv splits by negative scheme.
- **Reddit** (M0 1000, M1b / M2 300): M2 0.935 / 0.905, M1b 0.903 / 0.873, full graph 0.871 / 0.810 cross AUC. The halo
  stays ahead (+0.064 / +0.095 for M2). M0 at 300 epochs gives the same 0.870 / 0.807: it has plateaued.
- **arxiv** (M0 1000, M1b / M2 300): full graph 0.874 / 0.756, M1b 0.828 / 0.781, M2 0.825 / 0.763. The full graph wins
  with global negatives (+0.046); the halo wins with matched negatives (+0.025). The 100-epoch protocol under-trains M0
  on arxiv, so the arxiv gate result depends on the budget.
- **products** (M0 300, M1b 100, M2 300, chosen on validation): M2 0.958 / 0.927, M1b 0.955 / 0.921, full graph
  0.945 / 0.865 cross AUC. The halo stays ahead (+0.013 / +0.062 for M2). On validation the full graph peaks at 300
  epochs and drops at 1000 (0.897 / 0.771).
- **Full-graph training at 1000 epochs is unstable.** It collapsed once on Reddit (seed 1: 0.682; the same seed rerun
  gave 0.866), dropped on arxiv seed 2 (0.853 vs 0.883–0.888) and on products validation (0.897 vs 0.940 at 300).

## 5. What explains the results
- **The 1-hop halo carries the cross-unit signal.** Stage 3a alone is close to random on cross-unit links (0.53–0.70
  global). The halo lifts them to 0.83–0.96, at or above the full graph on Reddit and products.
- **The halo's I/O is the problem on Reddit only.** Reddit's 25 units are large and densely interconnected, so the halo
  is 4.3× the members. Keeping only the best-connected halo vertices (R = 1) keeps most of the gain: the Reddit
  validation curve falls smoothly from 0.935 (full) to 0.892 (R = 1), 0.858 (0.5) and 0.816 (0.25).
- **M5 gets most of the union gain at about 1× I/O.** On all three datasets it closes 69–137% of the union gap at
  1.04–1.13× bytes, with no halo at all. On cross-unit links it is clearly below the halo: Reddit 0.831 vs 0.868, products
  0.815 vs 0.955, arxiv 0.810 vs 0.825. Its union gain over Stage 3a still comes from the cross-unit pairs (products
  0.532 → 0.815, Reddit 0.704 → 0.831); intra-unit AUC stays where Stage 3a has it (products 0.996 vs 0.999, Reddit
  0.978 vs 0.963).
- **The local embeddings carry M5.** M3, the same decoder without h_local, falls below Stage 3a on every dataset
  (Reddit −0.121 matched; products −58% / −792%; arxiv −302% / −284%). The macro rows and features alone do not
  transfer.
- **Cut-edge supervision (M2 vs M1b)** helps on Reddit (+0.044 cross at 100 epochs), is neutral on products and slightly
  negative on arxiv's matched negatives.

## 6. Recommendation (for the journal extension of the BigData paper)
1. Make the halo budget a Phase 2 / 3 parameter (default R = 1). On graphs with large, densely connected communities
   it cuts what Phase 3 reads by about half, and the protocol keeps the link-prediction gain within the gate.
2. Report link prediction per stratum (intra / cross) and under both negative schemes. The union AUC with random
   negatives hides the cross-unit problem and the matched-negative behaviour.
3. Offer M5 as the cheap option where a halo is too expensive: one shared decoder after Stage 3a, about 1.1× its I/O.
   Use the halo where cross-unit links are the target.
4. Keep the full-graph baseline honest. Tune its budget on validation, report its instability, and say where it wins
   (arxiv, global negatives).
5. Next: measure the real Delta bytes of the halo per layout (Track B; the bytes here are an estimate), and run M5 / R = 1
   on EMR once AWS spend is approved.
